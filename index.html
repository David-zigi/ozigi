The firm

Green Gavel Legal is a Nigerian law firm providing comprehensive, solution-driven legal services to a broad spectrum of clients — corporate institutions, private companies, start-ups, non-governmental organisations, government agencies and private individuals.

We combine the energy of a forward-thinking firm with the depth of experience complex matters demand. Our lawyers are skilled practitioners with proven expertise in their respective practice areas and a reputation for diligence, analytical precision and results-oriented advocacy.

Our approach is hands-on and collaborative. We work closely with clients to understand their objectives, anticipate risk, and deliver proactive strategies that account for both legal and commercial reality. Our business law practice supports clients in establishing, managing and expanding operations within and outside Nigeria.

Our clients include government agencies, airlines, non-governmental organisations, banking and financial institutions, real estate developers and property investors, privately held and family-owned companies, and private individuals.
